Editing tips
  • Drag a band to move it. Arrow keys nudge the selected band.
  • Shift-click in the lane to add a band, or click a dashed ghost.
  • Double-click a band (or press Delete) to remove it.
  • Drag a lane sideways, or drag its left/right edges, to re-detect.
  • Drag the top/bottom limit lines to exclude membrane edges.

4 · Align & crop
